# Task 0 — Product Feedback

## Product

Simplify Money

## Mandatory actions completed

- Downloaded and used the Simplify Money application.
- Completed the available profile/setup flow.
- Referred three friends.
- Followed Simplify Money on the requested social channels.

## Feedback collected

### Friend 1

**What they liked**
- Liked the concept of automatically tracking transactions because it saves manual effort.

**What they did not like / concerns**
- Main concern was trust when the app reads financial messages.
- Wanted clearer information about what data is accessed and how it is used.
- Wanted an easy way to correct or report a transaction when categorization is incorrect.

### Friend 2

**What they liked**
- Liked that onboarding was fairly straightforward and did not take too long.

**What they did not like / concerns**
- Felt the profile/setup process could explain more clearly why certain information is needed.
- Felt the initial experience could do a better job of explaining what the app actually does before asking the user to complete everything.

### Friend 3

**What they liked**
- Found the idea of having financial activity in one place useful.
- Found the interface relatively easy to understand.

**What they did not like / concerns**
- Felt that some parts were not immediately obvious and required exploration.
- Suggested making navigation and labels more self-explanatory.

## Common themes

Three themes appeared across the feedback:

1. **Trust and transparency**
   Users want to understand what financial data is accessed and how it is used.

2. **Clearer onboarding**
   Users should understand the product's purpose and why information is requested before completing setup.

3. **Better discoverability and correction**
   Navigation and labels should be more self-explanatory, and users should have a simple way to correct or report incorrect transaction categorization.

## My observations

The feedback reinforced that transaction accuracy and transparency are particularly important for a financial product.

The Track experience makes automated transaction tracking the central value proposition, but that also means users need confidence in where transaction information comes from and whether the resulting categorization is correct.

This connects directly with the backend incident investigated in this assignment, where an available balance was incorrectly interpreted as a transaction amount.
